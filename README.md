# Demographic Data Visualization: Bandar Lampung Population Dashboard

![Dashboard](hasil.jpg)

## 1. The Problem

Dynamic population growth in Bandar Lampung brings significant challenges in urban development planning, public policy, and the equitable distribution of public facilities. Raw and complex demographic data often makes it difficult for stakeholders to formulate inclusive policies, creating a strong need for comprehensive and easy-to-understand data visualization.

## 2. My Approach

This project was developed during my internship at the Central Bureau of Statistics (BPS) of Bandar Lampung City, focusing on data exploration and dashboarding:

* **Data Processing:** Utilized Python (via Google Colab with Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn) to clean and process the population dataset from 20 sub-districts in 2023.
* **Visualization & Dashboarding:** Built an interactive dashboard using Google Looker Studio, featuring sub-district filters, gender distribution comparison charts, and a spatial distribution map (bubble map).

## 3. Results & Impact

* Successfully mapped Kemiling and Panjang as the sub-districts with the highest populations, providing quantitative signals for the government to prioritize public service capacity and infrastructure adjustments in those areas.
* The analysis proved the absence of a significant gender gap across the city (proportion of 50.58% male and 49.42% female), providing data certainty for the government to maintain the effectiveness of inclusive policies.
* Delivered a ready-to-use dashboard for relevant agencies to monitor demographic changes and support strategic decision-making processes.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Programming Language:** Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn)
* **Visualization & Dashboarding:** Google Looker Studio
* **Core Skills:** Data Visualization, Data Analytics

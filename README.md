# 🏙️ Demographic Data Exploration & Dashboard (BPS Bandar Lampung)

![Dashboard Preview](hasil.jpg)

## 1. Business & Policy Context
As the capital of Lampung Province, Bandar Lampung experienced rapid urban and population growth (increasing by 19.01% over the past decade). This rapid growth introduces significant challenges in urban planning, public policy execution, and public service distribution across sub-districts (*kecamatan*). Raw demographic data is often too complex for municipal decision-makers to interpret quickly, driving the need for an interactive, data-driven visualization platform.

---

## 2. Data & Process
* **Data Source:** Official 2023 Population Dataset from the Central Bureau of Statistics (BPS) Kota Bandar Lampung (covering 20 sub-districts).
* **Dashboard Development:** Built an interactive, single-page dashboard using **Google Looker Studio**, featuring sub-district slicers, gender distribution bar charts, stacked gender comparison charts, and a spatial bubble map displaying population density.

---

## 3. Analysis & Key Findings
* **Top Population Density:** **Kemiling** is the most populated sub-district with **86,300 residents** (50.5% male, 49.5% female), followed by **Panjang** with **74,858 residents**.
* **Lowest Population Zone:** **Enggal** records the lowest population count with **25,752 residents**.
* **Gender Ratio Equilibrium:** The citywide population exhibits a highly balanced gender distribution (50.58% male vs. 49.42% female), with slight variations in central sub-districts like Enggal, where the female population slightly exceeds the male population.

---

## 4. Actionable Public Policy Recommendations
1. **Infrastructure & Public Facility Scaling:** Prioritize capacity expansion for health services, schools, and public transport infrastructure in high-density growth zones (**Kemiling** and **Panjang**).
2. **Municipal Resource Allocation:** Utilize the spatial population density data to optimize regional budget distribution (*APBD*) and municipal service capacity based on actual demographic demand.
3. **Gender-Inclusive Policy Maintenance:** Continue monitoring gender distribution metrics periodically to ensure equitable access to public services, healthcare, and community empowerment programs across all 20 sub-districts.
